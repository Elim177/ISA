# Interview_Survey_Aggregate

Interview questions
The interview guide follows the question order used in the thesis and paper:
- Q1–Q4: Participant background.
- Q5–Q10: Definitions of anonymity, information participants wanted to protect, activities where anonymity mattered, interaction factors, and beliefs about multiple accounts.
- Q11–Q18: Perceived anonymity, its importance, profile pictures, profiles included on resumes, and multiple-account practices.
- Q19–Q26: Privacy-policy reading, reasons for not reading, estimated reading time, willingness to read, and statements considered important.
- Q27–Q31: Rankings of contextual integrity presentations, perceived helpfulness, and willingness to read the restructured policies.
Additional questions and prompts are retained in the guide's supplementary section.


Supplementary survey questions
The supplementary survey asks:
1. Do you have a GitHub account?
2. Do you have a Stack Overflow account?
3. Do you post questions on Stack Overflow?
4. Are you anonymous on Stack Overflow?
5. What is your self-identified gender?
The Word document includes the response options reported in thesis Table 3.3. For analyses of perceived Stack Overflow anonymity, the relevant subset is the 19 respondents who reported having a Stack Overflow account.
